**Mishkaah** is an open-source initiative dedicated to preserving foundational Islamic texts — the Holy Quran, classical Tafseer, canonical Hadith collections, daily Adhkar, classical primers (*Mutun*) and commentaries, historical biographies, and Arabic dictionaries — in a verified digital collection and a calm reading experience.

We **collect and verify** heritage texts from accredited sources; we never author, invent, or generate religious content.

---

### Public Standards & Trust Architecture

- **[Data Accuracy & Protection](./docs/data-accuracy-and-protection.md)**
  Covers the **Strict 5-Point Trusted Source Criteria**, the **Universal Two-Person Human Review Rule** (Adder + Mandatory Independent Human Reviewer), and the **5-Layer External Trust & Digital Sanad Architecture** (Direct Printed Page Witness + Archive Link, Honest 4-Tier Maturity Badges excluding quarantined/untrusted data from production builds, Immutable Two-Person Audit Trail, Cryptographic SHA-256 Ratchet Seals & Open Verifier, and Public Printed-Proof Errata Process).

- **[Public AI Safety Principles](./docs/ai-governance.md)**
  Explains our absolute prohibition on generative authoring of sacred texts, closed-world deterministic filters, and mandatory human review against physical printed witnesses.

- **[Brand Assets](https://github.com/mishkaah/brand)**
  Official vector marks, adaptive app icons, and visual identity guidelines.
