# Mishkaah Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned milestones and verified additions for the Mishkaah Open Islamic Corpus. All work follows strict scholarly verification standards against accredited critical editions and official institutional portals.

Curriculum priorities are scheduled to support our calm, reliable reading application for web and mobile.

### How Milestones Are Maintained

- **Verification First**: We complete multi-stage forensic verification of foundational texts before expanding into additional titles.
- **Accredited Sources**: Every entry is tied to an approved critical printed edition or official scholarly portal registered in `corpus/SOURCES_MANIFEST.json`.
- **Language Separation**: Arabic texts remain pure classical Arabic with full diacritics; translations are maintained in decoupled stand-off overlays.

To learn how to contribute or report verified textual notes, review our [Contribution & Verification Guide](https://github.com/mishkaah/.github/blob/main/docs/non-technical-contributing.md).
